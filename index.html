POWER AUTOMATE · EMAIL INTAKE
PROJECT 01

Shared Mailbox Intake & Sender Identification

Mabany Edris · Customer Excellence

An automated intake workflow that checks the sender's domain and determines whether the incoming email should receive a customer request-form response.

The challenge

The shared mailbox received emails from customers, prospects, employees, and partners. Manual sender verification made initial handling slower and could result in request forms being sent to the wrong people.

The solution

A Power Automate flow checks the sender domain when a new email arrives. A condition determines the next action: internal senders and partners follow the appropriate internal path, while external senders receive an email containing the request-form link.

Workflow

  • Email arrives in the shared mailbox.
  • Sender domain is checked.
  • A condition evaluates the sender category.
  • The flow terminates or sends the appropriate email.

Operational value

Reduces manual sender verification, prevents unnecessary form emails to employees and partners, and creates a more consistent first step for incoming customer requests.

POWER AUTOMATE · REQUEST ROUTING
PROJECT 02

Customer Request Management & Intelligent Routing

Mabany Edris · Customer Excellence

A structured request-management workflow that records form submissions, acknowledges receipt, and routes each request according to its project and type.

The challenge

One shared mailbox served multiple projects, departments, and request types. Manual distribution made ownership less clear and increased the risk of delayed, lost, or incorrectly assigned requests.

The solution

A routing matrix maps projects and request types to their responsible departments. A digital form feeds an automated workflow that records responses, sends acknowledgments, and evaluates routing rules.

Workflow

  • A customer submits a structured form.
  • The flow retrieves the response details.
  • The submission is logged in a data table.
  • A confirmation email is sent.
  • Routing rules identify the destination mailbox.

Operational value

Standardizes request intake, improves assignment ownership, reduces manual distribution, and creates structured records that support tracking and reporting.
